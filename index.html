About me

I am a Ph.D. student at the College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University, advised by Prof. Bo An. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Xidian University.

I work on post-training language-model agents. Most of my recent work starts from the same practical constraint: real agent logs are plentiful, but online rollouts, reward models, and task verifiers usually are not. Concretely, I am interested in

  • agent post-training from raw, unlabelled interaction trajectories;
  • credit assignment at the token and step level for tool-use agents;
  • deliberative search and iterative revision as an alternative to one-shot generation;
  • taking these methods into industrial decision-making systems.

I am always happy to talk about potential collaborations — feel free to reach out by email.

Publications

Under review, NeurIPS 2026

AgentBrew: Offline Tool-Use Agent Learning from Raw Real-World Trajectories

Zhiyi Lyu, Yewen Li, Longtao Zheng, et al.

AgentBrew post-trains tool-use agents from raw interaction logs with no online rollouts, external rewards, or task verifiers. Retrospective task inference plus PMI-based step-level credit assignment turns noisy trajectories into weighted SFT supervision.

Paper

WWW 2026

LBM: Hierarchical Large Auto-Bidding Model via Reasoning and Acting

Yewen Li*, Zhiyi Lyu*, Peng Jiang, Qingpeng Cai, Fei Pan, Bo An

Separating high-level reasoning from precise numerical action lets an LLM agent operate in continuous decision spaces; the reasoning module is trained with offline RL, avoiding simulator and real-world rollouts.

Paper

NeurIPS 2025

Let's Revise Step-by-Step: A Unified Local Search Framework for Code Generation with LLMs

Zhiyi Lyu, Jianguo Huang, Yanchen Deng, Steven Hoi, Bo An

Code generation as anytime local search over programs. A revision reward model ranks candidates by revision distance, guiding both hill climbing and genetic search on LiveCodeBench and TACO. In collaboration with Alibaba Tongyi Lab.

Paper

ICML 2025

Towards Efficient Online Tuning of VLM Agents via Counterfactual Soft Reinforcement Learning

Lang Feng, Weihao Tan, Zhiyi Lyu, Longtao Zheng, Haiyang Xu, Ming Yan, Fei Huang, Bo An

CoSo measures each token's causal influence on the executable action and concentrates exploration on action-critical tokens, with convergence and policy-improvement guarantees.

Paper

Preprint, arXiv 2024

Q*: Improving Multi-step Reasoning for LLMs with Deliberative Planning

Chaojie Wang*, Yanchen Deng*, Zhiyi Lyu, Liang Zeng, Jujie He, Shuicheng Yan, Bo An

Q* casts multi-step reasoning as heuristic search and learns a plug-and-play Q-value model to score the next reasoning step, guiding decoding without fine-tuning the LLM for the task. Evaluated on GSM8K, MATH, and MBPP. With Skywork AI.

Paper

NeurIPS 2023

Few-shot Generation via Recalling Brain-Inspired Episodic-Semantic Memory

Zhibin Duan, Zhiyi Lv, Chaojie Wang, Bo Chen, Bo An, Mingyuan Zhou

A variational structured memory that stores semantic concepts alongside sample-specific episodic memories, with structured recall for rapid adaptation to unseen generation tasks.

Paper
